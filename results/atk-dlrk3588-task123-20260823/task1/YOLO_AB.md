# Physical YOLO RR versus FP-RR A/B

## Controlled topology

VM1 is StarryOS with a 27.4 MiB initramfs and real ncnn/yolo11n assets. VM2 is
RT-Thread with a 10 ms periodic probe. Each VM has one vCPU and both are bound
to pCPU1 (`0x100`). StarryOS has host priority 89 and RT-Thread priority 90.

The RR and FP-RR host TOMLs differ only in one feature:

* control: `rr-scheduler`;
* treatment: `fp-rr-scheduler`.

The guest configurations, guest binaries, memory map, device configuration,
model, input, and probe duration are identical. A `virtio-net` device remains
present in both arms because the reused model rootfs configures `10.0.42.15`.
The test does not use loopback as a substitute for the real workload.

## Results

| Run | samples | mean ms | P99 ms | max ms | >1 ms | YOLO infer us |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| RR-01 | 300 | 4.879 | 32.373 | 53.373 | 152 | 1,567,580 |
| RR-02 | 300 | 4.714 | 29.995 | 39.034 | 153 | 1,569,971 |
| RR-03 | 300 | 4.734 | 30.176 | 38.707 | 143 | 1,568,298 |
| FP-RR-01 | 300 | 0.664 | 2.805 | 7.995 | 22 | 1,699,343 |
| FP-RR-02 | 300 | 0.684 | 1.497 | 13.401 | 18 | 1,654,579 |
| FP-RR-03 | 300 | 0.781 | 4.861 | 10.714 | 22 | 1,661,044 |

The median per-run P99 is 30.175625 ms for RR and 2.805333 ms for FP-RR:
10.7565x lower, or a 90.703% reduction. Median YOLO inference time changes
from 1,568,298 us to 1,661,044 us (+5.91%), so the tail-latency result is not
obtained by removing the inference workload.

Every log contains `PERIODIC LATENCY COMPLETE samples=300`, a completed real
`TASK3_INFER`, running VM state, and scheduler/vmexit counters. FP-RR logs also
contain nonzero `higher_priority_services` or `lower_priority_services`, which
proves execution entered the priority-aware scheduler path rather than merely
building it.

`derived/periodic-summary.csv` and the six per-run CSVs are generated by
`tools/analyze-task1-periodic.py`; the method validates a contiguous 0..299
sequence and uses nearest-rank percentiles.
