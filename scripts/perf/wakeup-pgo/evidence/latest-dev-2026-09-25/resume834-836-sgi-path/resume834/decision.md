The frozen benchmark window starts before the sender's `FUTEX_WAKE` and ends after the receiver returns to user mode. The forward path is futex bucket wake collection, `ThreadWakeBatch`, task lock and blocked-to-waking publication, target-rq enqueue and reschedule publication, a physical SGI, CPU1 IRQ acknowledgement and action, idle exit, scheduler selection/switch, and receiver syscall return. The target-rq publication must precede the doorbell, and controller acknowledgement must precede logical edge claim. A shortcut that moves either step across its ownership boundary has no correctness argument.
